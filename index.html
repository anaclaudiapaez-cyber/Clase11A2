Plans with going to New!

We use going to to talk about plans and intentions: things we decided before speaking.

Going to + base form
+I am going to study in Chile.
She is going to travel by bus.
We are going to visit the museum.
–I am not going to fly.
He isn't going to stay in a hotel.
They aren't going to come.
?Are you going to apply for a scholarship? Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
Where is he going to stay?

How to build it

  • subject + am / is / are + going to + base verb
  • I'm going to travel (not travelling, not travels)
  • Time words: tomorrow, next week, next year, this weekend, in July, soon

Past, habit or plan?

  • I went to Mendoza last year. (past)
  • I go to Mendoza every year. (habit)
  • I am going to go to Mendoza next year. (plan)

Preferences and comparisons

Preferences and opinions

  • I like / love / enjoy travelling by train.
  • I prefer the mountains to the city.
  • My favourite place is… · In my opinion… · I think…

Comparisons

  • short words: cheaper than, bigger than
  • long words: more comfortable than
  • irregular: good → better, bad → worse
  • as… as: The bus is as slow as the train.

Welcome to Villa Verde: a guide for exchange students

Villa Verde is a medium-sized city near the mountains. It is quieter than the capital, but it has a lot of cultural life: concerts, film clubs and a big music festival every March. The university is in the north of the city, opposite a large park. You can get there by bus, by bike or on foot. The bus is cheaper than a taxi, and it runs every ten minutes.

The international office is on the second floor of the main building, next to the library. Exchange students usually choose between a hostel, a student flat or a host family. A student flat is more comfortable than a hostel for a long stay, but a hostel is a good option for the first week because you can meet people easily. On your first day, you need to register at the international office. Bring your passport and your student card.

A message from Valeria

Hi Sofi! How are you? I'm writing from Mendoza, where I'm spending the weekend with my exchange group. We arrived yesterday afternoon after a long bus trip. At first I was tired, but when I saw the mountains I felt much better. We're staying in a hostel near the main square. It's cheaper than a hotel and it's very friendly: last night we had dinner with students from Brazil and Italy, and we played cards until midnight. Today we're visiting the campus and the library. Tomorrow we're going to take a bus to the mountains, and I'm going to take a lot of photos for you. I prefer the mountains to the city because they are quieter and more beautiful. Next semester I'm going to apply for a scholarship to study abroad for six months. Do you want to come with me? Write soon! Vale
